# News taste

Feeds slot 10 (news ranking) and slot 111 (steelman). Copy to `data/profile.md`
(gitignored) and make it yours. It is read fresh every morning, so edits take
effect the next day with no restart. With no profile the news is unranked.

The narrator reads the prose sections, so write sentences rather than a tag list
- the wording matters more than the keywords.

## Interested in

Board games, especially long strategy ones, and the people who design them.
Local news about the city: transport, openings, strange planning decisions.
Space missions, particularly the small unglamorous ones.
Genuinely strange stories: animals doing crimes, bureaucratic absurdity,
long-running hoaxes, things that turn out to have been wrong for decades.

## Not interested in

Crypto. Stock prices. Company funding rounds and acquisitions, unless the thing
being acquired is interesting on its own.

## Tone

Prefer the specific over the sweeping. A story about one weird object beats a
trend piece about a category. Bad news is fine if it is genuinely important.

## Keywords

Used **only** by the offline fallback ranker, which is keyword overlap and has no
reading comprehension. The narrator reads the prose above instead.

Kept separate because prose defeats the heuristic: "acquisitions, unless the
thing acquired is interesting on its own" put *interesting* in the anti-list.

liked: board-game, strategy, designer, transport, metro, planning, space,
mission, probe, satellite, hoax, bureaucracy, absurd, animal, escaped, debunked

disliked: crypto, bitcoin, nft, stock, shares, earnings, ipo, funding,
seed-round, acquisition, merger
